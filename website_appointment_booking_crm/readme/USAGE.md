1. A visitor opens ``/book/<slug>`` and picks (or the browser detects) a
   timezone with no slot in their working hours: a banner appears above the
   calendar.
2. **Request a custom slot** opens a short form: name, email, preferred
   two-hour window and notes.
3. On submit the visitor sees a confirmation banner and receives a
   confirmation email.
4. In **CRM > Leads**, the request appears as a lead tagged **Out-of-hours
   request**, without salesperson, with the details in its internal notes.
   Open a slot for the visitor and reply from there.
